# Standalone Cosmic Gauntlet Prototype

## 8K Cinematic Universe Amendment

The supplied Time Stone Galaxy image is the visual authority for every galaxy.

Each galaxy must reach that same quality level:

- artifact-first
- molten stone at the centre
- orbiting ecosystem around it
- huge scale
- cinematic space depth
- internal energy pressure
- information nodes orbiting the stone

Current target: a navigable cosmic universe, not a dashboard, graph, or flat overlay.

## Scale Rule

The full map must not fit comfortably in one view. The user should be able to start zoomed into one galaxy, zoom out and discover more galaxies, keep zooming out before seeing the full six-galaxy system, zoom into a stone and see orbiting resources, and focus/reset between galaxies.

This must feel like Google Maps for a cosmic AI system.

## Asset Rule

Create or acquire high-resolution assets or procedural equivalents. Document all downloaded/generated assets in `docs/assets/COSMIC_GAUNTLET_ASSETS.md`, including source, license, local path, resolution, compression format, and purpose.

## Stone Bar

- TIME: molten emerald infinity-stone core with green/yellow internal plasma veins.
- MIND: molten violet/gold cognition stone with thought nebula, neural arcs, and council satellites.
- SOUL: molten amber/orange memory stone with internal fire and knowledge pressure.
- REALITY: molten cyan/white electric stone with crystal lightning and device satellites.
- POWER: molten red/violet fortress stone with reactor pressure and containment.
- SPACE: blue tesseract cube where all pipeline lanes pass through.
- HUMAN GATE: golden authority crystal/core larger than all stones.
